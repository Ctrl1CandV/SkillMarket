# GB/T 7714 Reference Quick Guide (for thesis-cn)

> **Version status (officially supported):** GB/T 7714-2025, “Information and Documentation—Rules for Bibliographic References,” took effect on 2026-07-01 and **fully replaced** GB/T 7714-2015. Official metadata: std.samr.gov.cn, record w3O2P1Odh2Q=. Checked 2026-09-11; published 2025-12-02; not an identical adoption of ISO 690:2021.

> **Content gap (disclosed):** As of the check date, the official full text for the 2025 edition had not been obtained. All detailed rules below are based on the **2015 edition** and marked legacy_2015. Differences from the 2025 edition still need item-by-item verification. Until then:

## Author rules (legacy_2015; changes in 2025 edition pending verification)

- List all authors when there are three or fewer. For more than three, list the first three followed by “et al.” (Western-language sources) or the applicable “and others” form for Chinese-language sources.
- Western names: family name first, given-name initials, family name in uppercase, e.g. SMITH J A. Include the period in et al.
- Write Chinese authors' full names; abbreviate only as permitted above.

## arXiv and preprints (legacy_2015 treatment; 2025 rules for electronic resources/preprints pending verification)

Treat as electronic resources, not journals: use [EB/OL], the URL https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>, and an access date. Prefer the formally published version when available (conference/journal) and do not list the preprint twice.

## Common errors (legacy_2015 checklist; 2025 changes pending verification)

1. Wrong type identifier: conference paper marked [J], dissertation marked [M].
2. Conference entry missing // and proceedings title, listing only the conference city.
3. Missing period after et al., or using et al. instead of the Chinese-language form for a Chinese reference.
4. Missing page range or using total page count.
5. Mixed Chinese and English punctuation (half-width commas/periods in Chinese entries, or vice versa); follow the reference's language consistently.
6. Duplicate entry for the same work; deduplicate by DOI/title before checking format.
7. Electronic resource missing an access date in [YYYY-MM-DD] form.
8. Inconsistent volume/issue form; use year, volume(issue): pages, e.g. 2026, 12(3): 45-52.
